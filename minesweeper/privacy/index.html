Minesweeper 3D – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

Minesweeper 3D is made by True Alien Aid ("we", "us"). This page explains what information the app handles and why. The short version: we don't run any servers, we don't ask you to sign in, and we never see your personal information. The ads in the free version are provided by Google, and Google collects some data to show them.

What stays on your phone

The app saves your chosen board size, your best times and whether you've bought "Remove ads". This is stored on your device only and we never receive it. Uninstalling the app deletes it. If Android backup is turned on, your phone may include it in your personal Google backup.

Ads

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's advertising software in the app collects and sends to Google:

  • your device's IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location
  • how you interact with the app and ads, such as app launches, taps and video views
  • diagnostic information, such as app launch time and performance
  • device identifiers, such as your Android advertising ID and app set ID

This data is encrypted in transit. You can read how Google uses it at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your phone's settings.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before ads are personalised. You can change your choice at any time from Privacy settings in the app's menu.

Purchases

If you buy "Remove ads", the payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details. The app asks Google Play whether you own the purchase so it can switch ads off, including after you reinstall the app or move to a new phone.

What we don't do

  • We don't require an account or sign-in.
  • We don't access your contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location.
  • We don't sell your personal information.

Children

Minesweeper 3D is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
